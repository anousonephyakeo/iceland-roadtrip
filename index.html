⛽ Fuel & Discount Key Tag Guide

Your Blue Car Rental car comes with a **discount key tag** attached to the keys. Do not lose it! Use it to save at all **Olís and ÓB** gas stations.

⛽ Refueling Plan & Times:
  • Day 2 (Golden Circle) — ~02:00 PM: Fill up at Olís Reykholt (on Route 37, right after Friðheimar Greenhouse) or Olís Geysir.
  • Day 3 (South Coast) — ~02:45 PM: Fill up at Olís Vík (180 km from Reykjavik). Use the key tag for 7 ISK off fuel and grab lunch at the on-site Grill 66 to get 10% off food!
  • Day 4 (Reykjanes Loop) — ~05:30 PM: Top up at Olís Ánanaust (Ánanaust 13, 101 Reykjavík) right before return. It is only 800m (2 mins drive) from the return office, preventing the under-filled tank fee!
🎁 Discount Key Tag Benefits:
  • ⛽ **7 ISK OFF** every liter of fuel.
  • ☕ **Gourmet filter coffee for only 65 ISK** (standard is 450+ ISK).
  • 🍔 **10% discount** at Olís Restaurants (Grill 66, Lemon, ReDi Deli).
⚠️ Exclusion Rule: The discount tag is accepted at all ÓB/Olís stations EXCEPT the 8 lowest-price base stations (as their prices are already at a maximum discount):
  • • ÓB Arnarsmári · ÓB Bæjarlind · ÓB Fjarðarkaup · ÓB Hamraborg
  • • ÓB Skúlagata · ÓB Selfoss · ÓB Borgarnes · ÓB Akureyri

How to use the tag at fuel pumps:

  1. Activate: Hold the key tag against the sensor plate/reader near the screen first. Wait for confirmation.
  2. Payment Card: Insert your credit/debit card and enter your 4-digit PIN.
  3. Select Amount: Choose a set amount (e.g. 5,000 ISK) instead of "Fill Up" to prevent massive bank holds.
  4. Pump: Insert nozzle and fill.
  5. Receipt: Print receipt at the end.

Warning: Always check your fuel type before pumping! Green pump = 95 Gasoline/Petrol. Black pump = Diesel. Look at the cap sticker.

🛡️ Road Safety & Emergency

Iceland's roads can be dangerous. Check the weather and road conditions every morning without fail!

☁️ Weather: vedur.is 🛣️ Roads: road.is 🚨 Emergency Call: 112

Most Common Hazards:

  • 💨 Car Door Wind Damage: Hold the car door with two hands when opening! Winds can easily rip doors backward (not covered by standard insurance).
  • 🪨 Windshield Stone Chips: Slow down when passing oncoming cars on gravel sections.
  • 🐑 Sheep on Road: Slow down. Sheep run randomly and will dart in front of the car.
  • 🌉 Single-lane Bridges: The vehicle closest to the bridge has the right of way. Slow down and check.
