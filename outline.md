## Page 9 [content]
- **Title**: TIPS, APPS & WARNINGS
- **Content**:
  - 4 apps to download:
    1. PARKA — parking at Geysir and South Coast
    2. CHECKIT.IS — Thingvellir National Park parking
    3. VEDUR.IS — Iceland weather, check every morning
    4. ROAD.IS — live road conditions, check before every drive
  - WARNINGS:
    - Reynisfjara waves: sneaker waves come without warning, stay well back
    - Windshield gravel: most common insurance claim, drive slowly on gravel roads
    - Fill up petrol whenever possible, stations are far apart
    - Credit card PIN required at petrol stations — call bank before trip
    - Extreme winds can push car sideways, grip steering wheel firmly
    - Leave at 7 AM every driving day — avoids all tour bus crowds
